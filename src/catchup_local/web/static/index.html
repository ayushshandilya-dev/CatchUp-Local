How it works

Slow on purpose.

1

Parse deterministically

Timestamps, senders, replies, media, edits and unparsed lines are handled by code — not guessed by a language model.

2

Let the model triage

A small local model reviews candidate messages in fixed-size batches. Its only output is a label and, for tasks, a grounded owner and deadline.

3

Validate every claim

Items keep their source message. Ungrounded details are replaced with “unknown”. Adjacent duplicates merge; conflicts stay split.

4

Show the seams

Counts, warnings, refused claims and the original transcript are visible. If a batch fails, it is labelled as untriaged.

What this cannot do

Small model. Real limits.

  • It reads a chat export, not your live WhatsApp. Forwarded context and replies outside the export are invisible.
  • A 1–2B local model can still pick the wrong label. Evidence links let you check; they do not make the label infallible.
  • Owners and deadlines are conservative. “unknown” is a feature when the message does not say.
  • No winner is chosen in a disagreement. Both sides are shown with their sources.
  • Speed depends on your CPU. On an 8 GB Intel laptop, a full sample run is minutes, not seconds.
