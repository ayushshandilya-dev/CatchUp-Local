1. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
2. Point at the model badge: “This is Ollama running locally. No cloud key exists in this project.”
3. Click **Show the sample briefing**.
4. Read the derived summary. Point at the source chips and the line “Derived from validated items — not written by the model.”
5. Scroll to **Read these first**. Open the venue-status card, then the hall-closure card. “The tool did not pick a winner. Both are here.”
6. Open an action. Point at `owner · Priya` and `due · Friday 6pm`; open the source drawer and show the exact phrase in the message.
7. Scroll to **Evidence trace**. Show citations valid, candidates, deferred items, model calls and the warning about the instruction-like message.
8. Close with the limits section: “The model can still be wrong. The citations are the product.”
9. Optional: click **Run the sample live** and show the batch progress. Say the honest time: minutes on this 8 GB laptop.
